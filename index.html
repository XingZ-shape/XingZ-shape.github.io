About Me

I am Jianghai Yu (余江海), a Ph.D. student at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University under the supervision of Prof. Weijia Jia.

My research interests include Efficient AI, LLM Safety, Optimization, and Feature Selection. My current work focuses on efficient and trustworthy machine learning systems, with particular emphasis on large language model safety, jailbreak generation and defense, optimization-based learning frameworks, and high-dimensional feature selection.
